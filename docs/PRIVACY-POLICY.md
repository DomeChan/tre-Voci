# Tre Voci — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 30 June 2026_

Tre Voci is an audio-first, multilingual nursery-rhyme app for young children, built by an independent developer.

## We collect nothing.
Tre Voci does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no sign-in.** The app never asks for an email, name, or login.
- **No network calls, no tracking, no analytics, no advertising.** The app works fully offline; all songs are bundled in the app.
- **On-device only.** Your child's name (optional) and listening history stay on your device in local storage and never leave it.
- **No third-party SDKs** that collect data.

## In-app purchases
The optional "Support the Maker" tip jar uses Apple's In-App Purchase. Payments are handled entirely by Apple — Tre Voci never sees your payment details and stores nothing about a purchase beyond what Apple provides on-device.

## Children's privacy
Tre Voci is designed for children and complies with applicable children's privacy laws. Because the app collects no data at all, there is no children's data to protect, sell, or disclose.
